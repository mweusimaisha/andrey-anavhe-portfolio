📋 Overview & Objective

A high-energy countdown piece spotlighting 5 African athletes — across football, basketball, track, and combat sports — who are breaking records and shifting culture globally. Mix of rapid-fire stats, archival clips, and hype commentary.
Drive watch time and shares — optimized for YouTube + IG Reels cross-post

🎯 Target Audience & Tone

African & Afro-diaspora sports fans, 18–35, US / UK / Canada / West Africa
General sports fans curious about African representation in global sports
🔥 Hype 💪 Empowering 🎉 Celebratory 📖 Educational 😂 Comedic 🎭 Dramatic ⚡ Fast-paced 🤝 Conversational
Write like a fan, not a commentator. Culturally aware language — diaspora references, current slang where it fits naturally. Don't write for ESPN. Don't write for Twitter either. Find the middle: informed, direct, a little hype.

🎨 Creative Direction

Dark background, high contrast. WinzTV brand colors — black, gold, white. Bold typography. Dynamic motion graphics.
Fast cuts — avg 2–3 sec per clip in hype segments. Slow down for key stat reveals. Match cuts to beat where possible.
Afrobeats or Afropop instrumentals (royalty-free). High energy. Avoid generic ESPN-style orchestral beds.
WTV-019 "Top 10 African Goals" — pacing, graphics style, energy
WTV-021 "Burna Boy x Sports" — cultural tone, audience-aware writing
No generic stock footage. Do not use clips showing players in negative moments without context. No lazy "Africa is rising" framing.

📝 Script Outline / Shot Guide

Scriptwriter / host to flesh out from this skeleton
0:00–0:30 — Cold open: fast-cut hype montage of all 5 athletes. No narration, just music + crowd SFX.

0:30–1:00 — Host intro: "These aren't just athletes — they're rewriting what's possible." Tease the list.

🔁 Revision Protocol

  • ✅
    All feedback goes in the Monday.com task thread — not DMs, not voice notes, not verbal. One place keeps everything visible and nothing gets lost.
  • ✅
    Use timestamps on video feedback — "At 2:14 the lower third covers the subject's face" is useful. "The graphics feel off" is not. Specific notes get fixed. Vague notes get sent back.
  • ✅
    Watch the full cut before submitting notes — compile everything, then post once. That's one revision round. Drip-feeding notes across multiple messages stretches the cycle unnecessarily.
  • 🚫
    No new creative direction after the v1 is approved — if the concept and structure are signed off, v2 is for technical fixes and copy edits only. Structural changes restart the brief.
  • 🚫
    No approvals over voice note or casual message — approvals need to be confirmed in the task thread in writing. "Sounds good" in a DM doesn't count.
  • ✅
    Two revision rounds max per deliverable — if something needs a third round, we schedule a quick alignment call before work continues. Usually means the brief missed something.
